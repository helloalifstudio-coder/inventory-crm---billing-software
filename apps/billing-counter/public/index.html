Android 11 TV Counter Setup Guide

1. Keyboard, Mouse & Barcode Scanner: Plug your USB Mouse, Keyboard, and Barcode Scanner directly into the Android TV USB ports (or a small 4-port USB hub). They work plug-and-play.

2. Full-Screen App Mode: Open your Vercel URL in Chrome on the Android TV and tap Add to Home Screen to launch it with your custom app icon and zero browser bars.

3. Direct Thermal Printing on Android TV: Install the free RawBT app on the Android TV, pair your USB / Bluetooth / LAN 80mm thermal printer inside RawBT, and select RawBT ESC/POS on the left for instant 1-click receipt printing.
